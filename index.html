About Me

Better tracking. Smarter campaigns. More clarity.

I help local service businesses use Google Ads more effectively. My work covers campaign structure, keyword intent, ad messaging, conversion tracking, and ongoing optimization.

I believe good optimization starts with accurate data. Before making major campaign changes, I check whether calls and form submissions are being tracked correctly.

My goal is to help businesses understand where their budget goes and make better decisions based on meaningful results—not clicks alone.
